About Kamala Lounge

Where Cultures Converge on Every Plate

Nestled on Rue Novion in the vibrant neighborhood of Asnières-sur-Seine, Kamala Lounge represents a bold vision: to create a dining destination where the aromatic spices of Thailand, the delicate artistry of Japanese cuisine, and the bold flavors of Southeast Asia come together in perfect harmony.

Our kitchen is led by chefs who bring decades of combined experience from Bangkok street markets to Tokyo sushi counters. Every dish—from our hand-rolled sushi to our fragrant Thai curries—tells a story of tradition reimagined for the modern palate.

We believe great food should be accessible. That's why we've crafted lunch formulas perfect for Asnières-sur-Seine's busy professionals, alongside an evening menu designed for leisurely exploration with friends and family.

Our Promise

A Neighborhood Destination

We're proud to call Asnières-sur-Seine home. Since opening our doors on Rue Novion, we've become more than just a restaurant—we're a gathering place for food lovers, a quick lunch escape for local professionals, and a celebration venue for families.

Our weekday lunch formulas were designed with you in mind: bold flavors, quick service, and prices that make daily visits possible. In the evenings and weekends, we slow down, inviting you to explore our full menu and creative mocktails.

Whether you're craving a vibrant poke bowl, a comforting pad thai, or an adventure through our sushi menu, Kamala Lounge delivers an experience that keeps our guests coming back—as our 4.7-star rating from 590 reviews proudly shows.
