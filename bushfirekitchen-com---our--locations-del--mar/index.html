More Than One Kitchen

Del Mar is home base — but we're not just here

Bushfire Kitchen started in 2012 with a simple idea: comfort food made from clean, honest ingredients, without the wait or the price tag of a sit-down restaurant. That idea grew into multiple locations across Southern California.

This page is about our flagship at 2602 Del Mar Heights Rd, in the heart of Beachside Del Mar — but wherever you are in SoCal, there's likely a Bushfire Kitchen nearby serving the same bowls, plates, and empanadas.
