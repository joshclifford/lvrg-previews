Who We Are

A neighborhood bakehouse built around what you can't get anywhere else

Nectarine Grove started with a simple frustration: it's genuinely hard to find a cafe that treats gluten-free and organic ingredients as the standard, not the exception. So we built one — a place where paleo, vegan, keto, and health-conscious eaters can order anything on the menu without asking a dozen questions first.

Every quiche, bowl, and cold-pressed smoothie leaving our kitchen is made with intent — sourced with care, prepared fresh daily, and built to actually make you feel good, not just look good on Instagram.

You'll find us tucked into Leucadia in Encinitas and along Via De La Valle in Del Mar — two spots, one philosophy, and a full menu of coffee, breakfast, salads, bowls, sandwiches, and baked goods worth the drive.
