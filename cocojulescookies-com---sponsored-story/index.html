Inside the Adams Ave Bakery Turning San Diego On to Churro Cookies and Vegan S'mores

By There San Diego Staff  |  San Diego

Tucked into a stretch of Adams Ave in San Diego, Coco & Jules Cookies has built a name for itself as a cookie and cake shop that treats flavor development like an art form. This isn't a bakery coasting on the classics alone, though it does those well too. It's a shop where chocolate chip shares the case with churro, Mexican hot chocolate, and a vegan s'mores cookie that has become something of a calling card.

A Shop Built Around Flavor, Not Just Tradition

Every cookie shop claims to be different. What separates Coco & Jules is the willingness to actually push the flavor profile somewhere new, borrowing from churro carts and Mexican hot chocolate while still keeping a classic chocolate chip on hand for the purists. It's the kind of menu that rewards ordering a mixed box rather than picking just one.

Coco & Jules Cookies is a cookie and cake shop located in San Diego that specializes in handcrafted cookies and cakes with creative flavors.

The shop runs on two tracks that make it easy to fit into an actual day: a physical storefront at 3415 Adams Ave for anyone who wants to walk in and pick from the case, and an online ordering platform for same-day or next-day pickup. That second option matters more than it sounds, especially for anyone planning around a birthday, an office treat run, or a last-minute craving for something more interesting than a grocery store bag of cookies.

Worth Building a Trip Around

Cakes are part of the lineup too, alongside the cookies, which means Coco & Jules can cover both the small, spontaneous purchase and the bigger occasion order without sending anyone elsewhere. It's a practical kind of range: the same shop that hands you a churro cookie on a Tuesday can also handle the cake for Saturday's party.

None of this happens quietly. Coco & Jules has picked up a 5-star rating across 194 reviews, a strong showing for a neighborhood bakery, and the kind of number that suggests the flavor experiments are landing more often than not.

How to Do Your First Visit Right

For a first-timer, the move is simple: place an online order ahead of time for same-day or next-day pickup, especially if there's a specific flavor in mind, since Instagram-worthy items like the vegan s'mores cookie tend to move fast. Walking in cold works too, but ordering ahead means skipping straight to the good part.

Either way, the destination is the same. Coco & Jules Cookies sits at 3415 Adams Ave, and the full menu, along with online ordering, lives at cocojulescookies.com. Start there, pick your flavors, and let the pickup do the rest.
