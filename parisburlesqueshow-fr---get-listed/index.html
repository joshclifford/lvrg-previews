Paris Burlesque Show, Cabaret and Event Production: Bringing Art Deco Glamour to Paris and Beyond

Paris Burlesque Show was founded by Marina De Bono in 2021 as a creation house built around one idea: that a dinner and a show shouldn't be two separate things. The company builds immersive evenings where theater, burlesque, live music, circus arts and glamour all run through the same production, rather than a dancer act bolted onto a standard dinner service.

The team works out of 105 Rue de Courcelles in Paris, but the shows themselves travel: past productions have taken over the Riviera Palace in Monaco, Château de la Gaude, and the Casino de Paris, among other prestigious venues. Alongside the public cabaret evenings, Paris Burlesque Show designs bespoke corporate and private events, bringing in dancers, musicians, acrobats and magicians to match whatever the occasion calls for.

If you're planning a private or corporate event, or you just want a seat at one of the upcoming public shows, this is a company built to be reached out to directly rather than booked off a generic menu.

What to Know Before You Reach Out

Paris Burlesque Show runs two kinds of experiences: public dinner-shows at partner venues around France, each with its own date, dress code and theme, and fully bespoke corporate or private events built from scratch for a specific brief. For public shows, reservations run through the individual event's booking link. For a private or corporate event, the first step is contacting the team directly to discuss the venue, the performers and the shape of the evening.
