Our Story

Two continents. One table. Infinite possibilities.

At Adouna, we believe the best meals happen when cultures collide. Our kitchen bridges the warmth of Senegalese tradition with the precision of Asian technique — creating dishes that feel familiar yet completely new.

From the iconic thieboudienne to crispy rice paper rolls, every plate tells a story of culinary exploration. Located in the heart of Asnières-sur-Seine, we've become the gathering place for business lunches that impress, family dinners that linger, and celebrations that matter.
