What We Offer

A Destination for Every Gathering

Rated 4.6★ from 996 reviews, Adouna delivers exceptional dining experiences that blend West African warmth with Asian refinement.

Fine Dining

Experience elevated cuisine where Senegalese thieboudienne meets crispy rice paper rolls. Every dish tells a story of two continents, crafted with precision and passion.

Fusion Cuisine

Our unique culinary identity bridges West African traditions with Asian influences. Discover unexpected flavor combinations that honor both heritages with every bite.

Live Music Performances

Weekly live music transforms dinner into an event. Let the rhythm enhance your meal as talented performers fill our elegant space with soulful melodies.

Sunday Brunch

Join us Sundays from 11h to 16h for our celebrated brunch experience. A leisurely gathering of family and friends over our fusion-inspired morning specialties.

Private Events & Privatization

Host your milestone celebrations in our elegant space. From intimate gatherings to full venue privatization, we create bespoke experiences for your special occasions.

Business Lunch Venue

Impress clients and colleagues in our refined setting. Convenient to Asnières-sur-Seine, we offer the perfect backdrop for productive and memorable business meals.
