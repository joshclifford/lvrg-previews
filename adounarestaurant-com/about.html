Our Story

Our Philosophy

The Art of Culinary Fusion

At Adouna, we believe that the most beautiful stories are told through food. Our name, meaning "the world" in Wolof, reflects our mission: to bring together the bold, soulful flavors of West Africa with the delicate precision of Asian cuisine.

Every dish on our menu is a conversation between two rich culinary traditions. Imagine the comforting warmth of Senegalese thieboudienne meeting the crisp elegance of Vietnamese rice paper rolls. This is not fusion for novelty's sake—it's a celebration of shared ingredients, techniques, and the universal language of hospitality.

Our kitchen team, led by chefs trained in both traditions, sources the finest ingredients to create dishes that honor their origins while forging something entirely new.

Our Space

Elegant Warmth in Every Detail

Located at 14 Bis Avenue de la Paix in the charming neighborhood of Asnières-sur-Seine, Adouna welcomes you into a space that feels both refined and inviting—much like the food we serve.

Our interior balances contemporary elegance with touches that nod to both West African artistry and Asian minimalism. Warm lighting, thoughtfully curated textures, and an open layout create the perfect backdrop for meaningful conversations and memorable meals.

Whether you're joining us for a business lunch, a family dinner, or a special celebration, our team ensures every visit feels personal and every guest feels at home.
