Our Story

Born in Bridgeport, brewed with love

In 2018, proud Bridgeport residents opened The Stockyard Coffeehouse with a simple mission: create a space where neighbors become friends over really good coffee.

Named for the historic stockyards that once defined this Chicago neighborhood, we honor our community's working-class roots while celebrating its vibrant Latin culture through every signature drink we pour.

Whether you're grabbing your morning espresso before the L or settling in for a Saturday brunch, you're part of our Bridgeport family.
