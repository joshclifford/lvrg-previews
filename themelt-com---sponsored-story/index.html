How a San Diego Grilled Cheese Stand Turned Into One of the Best-Rated Burger Chains in the Country

By There San Diego Staff · San Diego

Tucked into a shopping center on El Camino Real in Carmel Valley, The Melt doesn't look like a chain that's earned some of the best ratings of any burger restaurant in the country. It looks like a neighborhood spot, the kind of place you duck into after soccer practice or on a Tuesday when nobody wants to cook. That's more or less the point. The Melt has built its name on melted cheese, done a handful of ways, done well, and it's spread from that first San Diego footprint out to multiple locations across the western United States, including stops as far-flung as SFO Airport and Long Beach.

Cheese, Done Several Ways

The menu reads simple, and that's by design. Meltburgers anchor the lineup, thick patties buried under a pull of melted cheese that's clearly the whole reason the place exists. From there it branches into crispy chicken sandwiches, a proper grilled cheese lineup under the Melted Classics name, and a mac and cheese that gets treated like a main course instead of a side. Salads and soup round things out for anyone who wants a lighter plate, and the milkshake menu is there for anyone who doesn't.

The Melt is a burger and comfort food restaurant chain specializing in premium melted cheese burgers, crispy chicken sandwiches, grilled cheese, and mac and cheese.

That focus is what separates The Melt from the drive-through burger stands lining every commercial strip in the county. There's no attempt to be everything to everyone. Comfort food, done with better ingredients than the format usually gets, is the entire pitch, and it's carried the brand to a 4.7 star rating across Google and Yelp, which puts it among the highest-rated burger chains in the country.

Built for How People Actually Eat Now

The Melt has clearly put real thought into getting food to people the way they actually want it in 2024, not the way a restaurant assumes they do. There's a mobile app built for faster ordering and better delivery pricing than the third-party apps offer, straightforward delivery for nights nobody wants to leave the house, and a catering menu for anyone feeding a group that's outgrown home cooking. Melt Rewards sits on top of all of it, handing out perks and free items to people who order enough to make loyalty worth tracking.

★ 4.7 (826 reviews) is a hard number to argue with in a category as crowded as burgers, and it's built one order at a time across every location the brand has opened, from the Carmel Valley original to the Long Beach spot to the counter tucked into SFO. A chain doesn't hold that kind of rating by accident.

What to Order First

First-timers do best starting with a Meltburger and picking a milkshake to go with it, since the shake lineup is deep enough to be its own reason to stop in. Anyone leaning vegetarian or just craving cheese without the beef should go straight for the grilled cheese. If there's a group involved, ordering a mac and cheese to split down the middle of the table isn't a bad instinct either.

The smartest move for repeat visits, though, is skipping the counter line altogether. Downloading the app gets faster ordering and lower delivery pricing than going through a third party, and joining Melt Rewards means the free items start stacking up before you'd expect. For a chain with locations scattered from San Diego to the Bay Area to Long Beach, that's the difference between treating it like a one-off stop and making it a regular habit. Either way, the full menu, hours by location, and ordering are all live at themelt.com.
