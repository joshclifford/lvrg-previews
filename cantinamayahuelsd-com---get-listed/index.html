About Cantina Mayahuel

Cantina Mayahuel brings a serious tequila and mezcal program to Adams Ave, paired with a Mexican restaurant menu built for lingering over a meal indoors or out on the patio. It runs first-come, first-served, so there's no reservation to plan around, just a table when you're ready and a bar list worth spending an evening exploring.

Alongside the dining room sits Casa Mayahuel, their own curated bottle shop for agave spirits, for anyone who wants to take a piece of the cantina home. The best way to experience it is simply to walk in, no reservation needed, and settle into an authentic tequila and mezcal night in North Park's neighboring stretch of San Diego.
