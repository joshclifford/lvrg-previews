Our Philosophy

Good Food & Good Times,
Thoughtfully Crafted

At Treehouse, we believe dining should invigorate both body and mind. Our chefs source the freshest seasonal ingredients to create menus that surprise and satisfy—from vibrant greens and hearty grains to artisan pizzas made with care.

Located in the heart of Sorrento Mesa at Pacific Heights Boulevard, we're proud to serve the hardworking community that surrounds us. Whether you're grabbing a quick latte or lingering over a long lunch, you're always welcome here.
