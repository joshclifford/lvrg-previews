Our Story

Bold Flavors of Authentic Panadería

A San Diego Kitchen Built on Family Recipes

Panchita's Kitchen and Bakery invites you to experience the rich flavors of authentic, handcrafted Mexican cuisine — the same commitment to craft that's made us a fixture of the North Park community. What started as a family bakery has grown into a full kitchen serving breakfast, lunch, and dinner alongside pan dulce and pastries made fresh, in-house, every day.

We're not a chain, and we don't cut corners. Every plate that leaves our kitchen reflects generations of Mexican culinary tradition — from our masa to our mole — served in a warm, family-friendly space where regulars become family and first-timers come back for more.

Our North Park location at 4414 University Ave anchors our roots in this neighborhood, with sister locations across San Diego carrying the same recipes and the same care into Golden Hill and Barrio Logan.
