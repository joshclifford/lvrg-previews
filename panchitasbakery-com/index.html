Our Story

Bold flavors, baked and simmered the honest way

Panchita's Kitchen and Bakery invites you to experience the rich flavors of authentic, handcrafted Mexican cuisine — from sizzling breakfast plates to golden conchas pulled straight from the oven.

Tucked into North Park at 4414 University Ave, we've built our reputation one order at a time — patio regulars, drive-thru commuters, and families booking us for their next big celebration.
