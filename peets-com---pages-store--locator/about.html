OUR STORY

Where Craft Coffee Began

In 1966, Alfred Peet opened a small coffee shop on Vine Street in Berkeley, California. He brought with him a lifetime of knowledge from his family's coffee trade in Holland, and a conviction that Americans deserved better coffee.

His approach was revolutionary: sourcing the finest beans directly from origin, roasting them darker and deeper than anyone else, and selling them fresh — never more than ten days old. Word spread. Lines formed. A movement began.

Today, from our San Diego Coffeebar at 7845 Highlands Village Pl to homes across the nation, we remain committed to Alfred's original vision: exceptional coffee, crafted with care, shared with intention.
