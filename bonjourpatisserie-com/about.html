Our Story

French pastry craft, made for San Diego's biggest moments.

Bonjour Patisserie is a French bakery and patisserie in the heart of San Diego, dedicated to the kind of elegant, precise baking that turns dessert into an occasion. From delicate viennoiserie to large-format custom cakes, everything is made by hand at our shop at 320 W Cedar St Unit 101, San Diego, CA 92101.

We built our reputation on the small things done right — laminated croissant dough, seasonal fruit tarts, and cakes that hold up structurally and visually for weddings and large events. That attention to craft is why Bonjour is recognized as a trusted wedding and event vendor across San Diego.

Bonjour has also become known for creative twists on the classics — including the crookie, a croissant-cookie hybrid that's earned recommendations well beyond San Diego. It's a small signal of a bigger philosophy: respect the French technique, but don't be afraid to have fun with it.
