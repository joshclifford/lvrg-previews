Our Story

Rustic blue and white charm, baked fresh since the first morning in DUMBO

Maman brings a French countryside sensibility to Brooklyn's waterfront — exposed brick, soft blues, and the quiet hum of a bakery that never stops working. Every morning begins the same way: laminated dough, fresh soups, and coffee poured for guests who came for the view and stayed for the croissant.

Beyond the counter, Maman hosts the moments that need a little more care — private dinners, corporate gifting, full-service catering, and a published cookbook that brings the recipes home. If you've been meaning to book something here, this is the easiest way to finally do it.

What We Serve

From morning pastry to full-scale catering

Breakfast, Salads & Tartines

Pastries and baked goods each morning, open-faced tartines, hearty salads and soup, coffee and tea — all served dine-in, pickup, or delivered across the neighborhood.

Online Ordering & Delivery

Order ahead for pickup or have it delivered — plus corporate gifting boxes and our cookbook, for anyone who wants Maman on their own shelf.

Private Events & Catering

DUMBO's waterfront setting, made private. Full catering menus and event space for parties, showers, and company gatherings, large or small.
