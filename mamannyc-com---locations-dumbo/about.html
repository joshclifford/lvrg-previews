Our Story

Who We Are

A French countryside café, rooted in Brooklyn

Maman is a French-inspired bakery and café set along the cobblestones of DUMBO, where rustic blue-and-white charm meets the energy of one of Brooklyn's most beloved waterfront neighborhoods. From the first light of morning pastries to the quiet hum of an afternoon coffee, our rooms are built to feel like somewhere you'd linger — not just pass through.

We bake daily, build our menus around breakfast, tartines, salads and soup, and pour coffee and tea that's meant to be savored, not rushed. Beyond the counter, Maman hosts private events and catering across its locations, publishes its own cookbook, and ships corporate gifting for teams who want a taste of the café without leaving the office.

When Maman opened its doors at 66 Water St, it marked the brand's next chapter in a neighborhood recognized as one of the city's most active retail corridors — a fitting home for a café built on craft, warmth, and detail.
