Our Services

From morning pastries to full-scale private events, in the heart of DUMBO.

Everything Under One Blue Awning

Maman at 66 Water St brings French countryside warmth to Brooklyn's waterfront — a full café menu by day, a catering and private events partner year-round. Whether you're stopping in for a croissant or planning a celebration for fifty guests, here's everything we do, all in one place.

Breakfast & Brunch

Eggs, grain bowls, and café classics served daily from kitchen open at 8am — a gentle start to a DUMBO morning.

Pastries & Baked Goods

Flaky croissants, seasonal tarts, and bakery classics made fresh each morning for walk-ins and standing orders alike.

Sandwiches & Tartines

Open-faced tartines and hearty sandwiches built for lunch on the waterfront, dine-in or packed to go.

Salads & Soup

Lighter plates built from seasonal produce, rotated with the kitchen's daily rhythm — simple, honest, well-balanced.

Coffee & Tea

A proper café counter — espresso drinks, drip coffee, and tea service for lingering over a pastry or two.

Online Ordering

Order ahead for pickup and skip the counter, or have Maman delivered directly to your DUMBO office or apartment.

Delivery & Pickup

Flexible options across our locations — whether it's a quick coffee run or a full lunch order for the team.

Corporate Gifting

Curated pastry and gift boxes for clients, teams, and holidays — a French touch of thanks delivered with care.

Cookbook Sales

Bring the Maman kitchen home with our published cookbook, available in-café and through our team.
