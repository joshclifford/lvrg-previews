Our Story

Serving Mission Valley Since 1996

A Mission Valley Institution

Nearly Three Decades of San Diego-Style Flavor

Since 1996, Sombrero Mexican Food has been a fixture of Mission Valley, dishing out the kind of San Diego-style Mexican food that keeps regulars coming back week after week. Tucked near the Fashion Valley and Mission Valley malls, we've grown into a go-to lunch spot for local businesses, shoppers on a break, and families craving something honest and satisfying.

We didn't reinvent Mexican food — we just made sure to do the local classics right. Our Carne Asada Fries and Fish Tacos have become neighborhood favorites for a reason: fresh ingredients, generous portions, and recipes that haven't cut corners since day one.

What Makes Us Different

Rooted in the Neighborhood, Built for Speed

Sombrero isn't a chain trying to feel local — we are local. Mission Valley has watched us grow from a small counter operation into a daily staple, open early for breakfast and late for the post-shopping, post-work crowd. Whatever the hour, from 7AM to midnight, we're here.
