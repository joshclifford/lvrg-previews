Our Story

Where Food, Community & Opportunity Meet

In the heart of Normal Heights, MAKE Projects is more than a cafe — it's a launchpad for refugee and immigrant women building new lives in San Diego.

A Hyperlocal Movement for Economic Empowerment

MAKE Projects began with a simple belief: that every woman deserves the opportunity to build a sustainable career and provide for her family. What started as a vision has grown into a thriving social enterprise that combines urban agriculture, culinary training, and community connection.

Our unique model integrates a working urban farm with a neighborhood cafe, creating real-world job training environments where program participants gain hands-on experience in food service, agriculture, and hospitality.

What Sets Us Apart

We're not just serving food — we're cultivating careers, growing community, and harvesting hope.

Job Readiness Training

Our 12-week program accelerates refugee and immigrant women into living wage career pathways with hands-on training and professional development.

Sustainable Agriculture

Our urban farm grows sustainably sourced produce that supplies our cafe and community through CSA boxes, connecting farm-to-table in Normal Heights.

Community Connection

From graduate gatherings to volunteer opportunities, we build lasting relationships that support women long after they complete our program.

A Social Enterprise Model That Works

Every meal served at MAKE Cafe directly supports our mission. When you dine with us on Felton Street, you're not just enjoying delicious food — you're investing in a woman's future.

Featured by WhatNow San Diego as one of the neighborhood's must-visit destinations, MAKE Cafe has become a beloved gathering spot where great food meets greater purpose.
