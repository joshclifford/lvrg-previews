Our Programs & Services

From farm to cafe to career — discover how MAKE Projects creates pathways to economic empowerment for refugee and immigrant women in Normal Heights.

What We Offer

Three Pillars of Impact

Every service at MAKE Projects is designed with purpose: to train, employ, and empower refugee and immigrant women while building community in San Diego.

Career Training

Our 12-week job readiness program and ongoing training develop real-world skills that lead to living wage careers.

Farm & Food

Our urban farm and cafe create sustainable food systems while providing hands-on training and employment opportunities.

Community

Graduate gatherings, volunteer opportunities, and community events keep our network strong and growing.

Flagship Program

12-Week Job Readiness Employment Program

Our comprehensive program accelerates refugee and immigrant women into living wage career pathways. Participants gain hands-on experience in food service, urban agriculture, and professional workplace skills.

  • Professional skills training and workplace readiness
  • Hands-on experience in cafe and farm operations
  • Career pathway support and job placement assistance
  • Ongoing graduate community and support network

Cafe Experience

MAKE Cafe — Farm-to-Table Dining

Located at 4712 Felton Street, off of Adams Ave in Normal Heights, MAKE Cafe serves breakfast, lunch, and brunch featuring sustainably grown produce from our urban farm. Every meal supports our training program.

Sustainable Agriculture

Urban Farm & CSA Boxes

Our urban farm provides sustainably grown produce that supplies the cafe and supports our CSA (Community Supported Agriculture) box program. Subscribers receive fresh, locally grown vegetables while directly supporting our mission.

Beyond Training

Community & Continued Growth

Our commitment doesn't end at graduation. We provide ongoing support, community events, and career development opportunities for all program alumni.

Graduate Gatherings

Regular community events bring together program graduates, staff, and supporters to celebrate achievements, share resources, and strengthen connections within the MAKE family.

Training & Career Development

Ongoing skill-building workshops, career coaching, and professional development opportunities help graduates continue advancing toward their career goals.
