Our Mission

More than a cafe. A launchpad for new beginnings.

MAKE Projects exists at the intersection of food, agriculture, and community empowerment. We believe that economic independence is the foundation of dignity — and that starts with opportunity.

Our 12-week program doesn't just teach skills. It builds confidence, creates connections, and opens doors to living-wage careers for refugee and immigrant women who've already overcome so much.
