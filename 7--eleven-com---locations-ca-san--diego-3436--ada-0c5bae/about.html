Who we are

7-Eleven is the convenience store name people already trust — bakery items, pizza, hot foods, roller grill favorites, coffee, energy drinks, Slurpee, Big Gulp, candy, ice cream, and beer and wine, all under one roof. Add in 7NOW delivery, the 7REWARDS loyalty program, mobile checkout, and fuel services, and this isn't just a corner store — it's a full stop-and-go hub.

Our San Diego location at 3436 Adams Ave carries that same standard: reliable stock, fast service, and everyday essentials without the wait. We also accept SNAP/EBT, because convenience should be accessible to everyone who walks through the door.

As Yelp put it plainly: "it's a pretty standard 7-Eleven" — and we take that as a compliment. Consistency is the whole point.

What makes this location different

We know the biggest frustration with finding a 7-Eleven online isn't the brand — it's the details. Hours, contact info, current stock. That's exactly what this page exists to fix for our Adams Ave store.
