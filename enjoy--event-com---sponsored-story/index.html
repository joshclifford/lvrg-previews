Inside the Levallois-Perret Agency Turning Corporate Events Into Something Worth Remembering

By There San Diego Staff · San Diego

Enjoy Event is a French event management agency built around a simple idea: the companies people trust with their brand deserve an event partner who treats every detail like it matters. Based at 6 Rue Collange in Levallois-Perret, just outside Paris, the agency has spent its career producing conventions, product launches, seminars, galas and incentive programs for names that don't take chances on execution, L'Oréal, Pierre Fabre and BNP Paribas among them.

That client list tells you something about how the agency operates. Luxury and consumer brands don't hire an event partner for a single flashy night, they hire one they can bring back year after year, across markets and formats. Enjoy Event's portfolio reflects that kind of repeat trust: press events, influencer activations, product training sessions, team-building days and the increasingly common hybrid or fully digital gathering, all run by the same team.

A Team Built Around the Full Life of an Event

What separates an agency like this from a vendor is range. Enjoy Event doesn't just decorate a room, it manages conventions from concept through execution, builds incentive programs meant to motivate a sales team, stages product launches designed to make headlines, and handles the entertainment and animation that turns a corporate gathering into something people actually talk about afterward. Blind test activities and themed entertainment show up alongside more formal press events and galas, which says a lot about an agency willing to be serious and playful in the same season.

Enjoy Event creates memorable experiences for luxury and consumer brands, handling everything from conventions and press events to galas, team-building and hybrid digital events.

That breadth is the whole point. A brand planning a New Year gala doesn't want to coordinate one company for the venue, another for entertainment and a third for the press list. Enjoy Event's model is built so a single point of contact can carry a project from the first planning call through the last guest leaving the room, whether that room is in Paris or the event is happening across screens for a distributed team.

Why the Work Holds Up

Digital and hybrid events have become a real specialty rather than an afterthought, which matters for any brand still figuring out how to run a launch or a convention for an audience split between a physical venue and a screen. It's a harder problem than it sounds, and it's become one of the areas where Enjoy Event's experience with major consumer and luxury brands shows most clearly.

The agency currently holds a rating of ★ 5.0 (1 review), a small but clean data point for a firm whose real reputation lives in the brand names on its client roster and the years of repeat work behind them.

For a company weighing its next convention, seminar or incentive trip, the sensible first move is a conversation rather than a guess. Enjoy Event works from a custom proposal model, meaning the scope, format and budget get shaped around the actual event rather than a fixed package, which is worth knowing before reaching out.

If a brand launch, incentive program or corporate gala is on the calendar, the agency's own site at enjoy-event.com is the place to see more of the work and start that conversation directly.
