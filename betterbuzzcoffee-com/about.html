Our Story Started with a Single Cup

San Diego's beloved small batch coffee roaster since 2002 — where California surf culture meets specialty coffee craftsmanship.

Est. 2002

Born in San Diego, Brewed for Community

What began as a single coffee shop has grown into a San Diego institution with multiple locations throughout the city. But no matter how much we've grown, our mission remains the same: craft exceptional coffee that brings people together.

Every batch of beans we roast is small — intentionally so. We believe that great coffee isn't about volume; it's about attention to detail, carefully selected origins, and the patience to get every roast just right.

From our specialty drinks to our signature egg bites, everything we serve is designed to make your morning a little brighter and your afternoon a little more productive.
