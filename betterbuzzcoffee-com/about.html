Our Story Started with a Single Cup

Born in San Diego, roasted with love since 2002.

Since 2002

Small Batch. Big Heart.

Better Buzz Coffee started with a simple dream: to bring exceptional, small-batch roasted coffee to the sunny shores of San Diego. What began as a single vision in 2002 has grown into a beloved community staple across multiple locations.

We're not just another coffee shop. We're your neighbor, your morning ritual, your afternoon pick-me-up. Every bean we roast tells a story — from carefully sourced origins to the moment it hits your cup.

Our laid-back California beach lifestyle meets an unwavering commitment to quality. We believe that the best coffee comes from passion, precision, and a little bit of that San Diego sunshine.
