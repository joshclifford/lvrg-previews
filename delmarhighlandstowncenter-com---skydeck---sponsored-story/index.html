Del Mar's Rooftop Food Hall Finally Puts San Diego's Best Chefs Under One Roof

By There San Diego Staff · San Diego

Up on the top level of Del Mar Highlands Town Center, past the shops and the movie theater, there's a stretch of San Diego that plays by different rules. Sky Deck Del Mar isn't one restaurant with one menu and one host stand. It's a collective, a run of chef-driven kitchens, a craft cocktail bar, and three of the city's best-known craft breweries, all sharing the same open-air rooftop with views that stretch out over the coast.

The design does a lot of the talking before the food ever arrives. Sky Deck leans into a maritime industrial look, all exposed steel and warm wood and open sightlines, built for the kind of evening where you start at one bar and end up at another without ever leaving the building. It's a layout that rewards wandering, which is exactly the point.

One Address, an Entire Night Out

What separates Sky Deck from a typical restaurant row is that nobody's making you choose just once. The lineup covers fine dining restaurants, a craft cocktail bar built around serious drink-making, and three of San Diego's top craft beer operators sharing deck space with panoramic views. Order Italian at one counter, walk twenty feet for a cocktail, then settle into a brewery seat for the last round. Del Mar has plenty of good individual restaurants. It doesn't have many places built like this one.

Sky Deck Del Mar at Del Mar Highlands Town Center offers an elevated dining experience with award-winning chefs, a luxury cocktail bar, and three of San Diego's top craft beer operators.

Chef-Driven, Not Chef-Adjacent

The kitchens here aren't an afterthought to the bar program. Sky Deck bills itself around the creations of award-winning chefs, and the roster backs that up, with Italian dining and wood-fired pizza sitting alongside the kind of craft brewery food that's built to pair with a flight rather than just fill a seat. Brunch, lunch, and dinner all run here, which means the deck has a different personality depending on when you show up, sun-drenched brunch crowd on the weekend, dinner-and-drinks energy once the sun drops.

Sky Deck also handles private events, which quietly makes it one of the more useful venues in this part of the coast. A rooftop with this much square footage, this many food and drink options, and views this wide isn't a common combination for a birthday dinner, a work gathering, or a milestone celebration that needs more than one restaurant can offer on its own.

Worth the Trip Up

The Sky Deck Del Mar reputation isn't built on marketing copy alone. It carries a 310-review track record at ★ 4.6, a strong showing for a venue juggling multiple kitchens and bars under one roof, and that kind of consistency across so many separate operators says something about how the whole deck is run.

First time up there, the smart move is to go without a fixed plan. Walk the deck once before you sit anywhere, see which kitchen has the shortest line and which bar has the view you want, then pick your starting point. Reservations matter here more than the layout suggests, since each restaurant runs its own book and its own hours.

The easiest way to lock in a table before a Del Mar evening is to go straight to the source. Visit delmarhighlandstowncenter.com/skydeck to see the current restaurant lineup and get a reservation on the calendar before the deck fills up.
