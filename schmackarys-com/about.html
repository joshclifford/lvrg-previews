Who We Are

The Official Cookie of Broadway, now baking in the Theater District

Schmackary's started as a scrappy little cookie shop with a big personality and an even bigger flavor lineup. We earned the nickname NYC's "Official Cookie of Broadway" one warm, oversized batch at a time — and we brought that same energy west when we opened our doors in San Diego's own Theater District, right near Union Square.

We're not a bakery that plays it safe. Our flavors rotate constantly, our seasonal drops sell out fast, and our kitchen never stops experimenting. If you've been chasing that same-old chocolate chip your whole life, you're in the right place — and about to be very pleasantly surprised.
