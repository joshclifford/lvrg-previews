Fresh Where You Already Are

You won't find us tucked behind a counter waiting for a lunch rush. Our fridges live inside the places people already move through all day — airports, hospitals, university halls, offices, and the Park Base break room at San Diego Zoo Safari Park. Every meal is prepared fresh in our Chicago kitchen and restocked daily, so what you see in the case is what's actually available right now, no subscription, no waiting on delivery windows.

Ordering, Made Simple

We know the extra tap to a separate app can feel like friction when you're standing in front of a fridge with a hungry crowd behind you. That's why the whole point of every kiosk — including the one at San Diego Zoo Safari Park — is to get you from "hungry" to "eating" in under a minute. Tap the screen, pay, walk away with a fresh meal.

Prefer to plan ahead? Reserve through the mobile app before you even arrive at the Park Base break room, so your meal is waiting the moment you get there.
