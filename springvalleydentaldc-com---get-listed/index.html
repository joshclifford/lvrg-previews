Spring Valley Dental: Family Dentistry in Spring Valley Built Around Personal Care

Spring Valley Dental is a family-focused dental practice in Washington, D.C., led by Dr. Priya Grewal and Dr. Steve Philips. Their office sits in the Spring Valley neighborhood and covers everything from routine cleanings to restorative, cosmetic, and emergency dentistry, with an approach built around minimally-invasive treatment and getting to know each patient rather than rushing them through a chair.

The practice describes itself as a family that happens to practice dentistry rather than a clinic that happens to see families. New and returning patients are encouraged to schedule a cleaning and get started with a plan built around their own history and needs.

What They're Known For

What to Know Before You Reach Out

Spring Valley Dental works with patients of all ages, from first cleanings to more involved restorative and cosmetic work, and positions itself as a dental home for the whole family rather than a single-visit provider. Office hours run Monday through Friday with varying hours each day, and the practice is closed weekends. Scheduling is handled online or by phone, making it straightforward for first-time visitors to get on the calendar.

In Their Own Words

We believe that good oral health leads to a better quality of life, and want to be your dental advocates. We specialize in preventative, restorative, cosmetic, and emergency dentistry.

Get in Touch
