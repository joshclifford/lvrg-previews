Our Story

A family kitchen turned San Diego staple, serving three neighborhoods with the same warmth we started with.

Bold Flavors, Family Roots

Authentic Panadería in the Heart of San Diego

Panchita's Kitchen and Bakery invites you to experience the rich flavors of authentic, handcrafted Mexican cuisine — a full menu of breakfast, lunch, and dinner favorites, paired with the artisanal pastries and desserts that first put us on the map.

What started as a single panadería has grown into a beloved presence across North Park, Golden Hill, and Barrio Logan — each location shaped by the neighborhood it serves, but all rooted in the same recipes and the same family pride.

Traditional Mexican comfort food, made with a modern twist, and served the way it should be — fresh, generous, and made to be shared.
