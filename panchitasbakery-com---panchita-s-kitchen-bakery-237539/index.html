Our Story

Bold flavors of authentic panadería, right in your neighborhood

Panchita's Kitchen and Bakery has served North Park, Golden Hill, and Barrio Logan with handcrafted Mexican comfort food and artisanal pastries since 1983. Breakfast, lunch, dinner, and dessert — all made the way it's always been made, with a modern touch.

We built our reputation on drive-thru convenience and patio mornings, but there's one thing we still haven't fixed: you can't order online. Yet. Until then, we're just a phone call — or a walk-up — away.
