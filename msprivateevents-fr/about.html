Who We Are

Passionate About Perfection

MS Private Events was born from a deep passion for creating meaningful celebrations. Based in the elegant 8th arrondissement of Paris, we specialize in crafting weddings and private events that reflect the unique personalities of our clients.

Our expertise spans across cultures and traditions—from intimate Parisian ceremonies to vibrant multicultural celebrations. We've proudly orchestrated events that blend Indian, Antillean, and French traditions, creating experiences that honor heritage while embracing modern elegance.

Every event we touch is handled with meticulous attention to detail, ensuring that your special day unfolds exactly as you've dreamed—and beyond.
