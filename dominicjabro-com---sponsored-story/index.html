Downtown Dealmaker: Inside Dominic Jabro's Approach to San Diego Real Estate

By There San Diego Staff  •  San Diego

From a high floor on B Street in the heart of Downtown, Dominic Jabro works a territory that stretches well past his own zip code, covering North San Diego, Carmel Valley, Rancho Peñasquitos, 4S Ranch, Del Sur, Solana Beach and the coastal stretch beyond. He is a licensed real estate agent who has built his practice around luxury homes and investment properties, and around the idea that a real estate deal should not be the most stressful thing a client does all year.

San Diego County is not one market, it is a dozen of them stitched together, and that is where the office at 750 B Street earns its keep. A buyer chasing a view lot in Solana Beach has almost nothing in common with an investor eyeing a multi-family building downtown, and Jabro's practice is built to hold both conversations at once, with the market knowledge to back up either one.

A practice built for both sides of the table

On the residential side, that means the full arc of buying and selling: seller services, buyer services, relocation help for people landing in San Diego from somewhere else, and a market and strategy service that treats pricing as a conversation rather than a guess. On the investment side, it broadens out considerably, into retail spaces, office spaces, multi-family properties, development land, industrial buildings, restaurant locations and medical office space. Few solo agents carry a services list this wide, and fewer still carry it comfortably.

That range shows up most clearly in the corners of the business that general-practice agents tend to hand off. Jabro handles 1031 exchange services directly, works with clients on wealth management strategy tied to their real estate holdings, manages divorce related real estate solutions with the discretion those situations require, and offers ongoing property management for owners who want the return without the second job. There is also a residential care facilities specialty, a niche corner of commercial real estate that takes real familiarity with the licensing and use requirements involved.

Dominic Jabro is a dedicated real estate agent specializing in luxury homes and investment properties throughout San Diego County, delivering seamless, stress-free real estate experiences tailored to each client's unique goals.

Why the details end up mattering

A lot of that difference comes down to transaction support, the unglamorous work of keeping a deal from falling apart between offer and closing. Contracts, disclosures, deadlines, inspection negotiations, lender coordination: it is the part of a sale nobody posts pictures of, and it is usually the part that determines whether a client walks away from the closing table relieved or exhausted. Jabro's transaction support service exists specifically to carry that weight so the client does not have to.

The commercial side of the business runs on that same instinct. A retail buildout in North Park, a restaurant location search along the coast, or a development land parcel in North San Diego all call for different due diligence, and Jabro's background across office, industrial, multi-family and specialty commercial property means he is not learning the asset class on the client's dime.

For anyone starting the process, the entry point is simple: a private consultation, in person or by phone, to talk through the goal before talking through the listings. Buyers walk in with a wish list, sellers walk in wondering what their property is actually worth in this market, and investors walk in with a return they are trying to hit. Jabro's site, dominicjabro.com, lays out the full range of services along with a way to request a home valuation directly.

Jabro currently holds a 5-star rating across 40 reviews, a track record built one closing at a time across a county that does not make closings easy. For San Diegans weighing a move, a sale, or a first investment property, that combination of range and follow-through is worth a conversation before it is worth a listing.
