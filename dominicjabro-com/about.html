About Dominic Jabro

A Real Estate Practice Built on Strategy, Discretion, and Results

Dominic Jabro is a licensed California real estate professional serving Downtown San Diego and the North County corridor — guiding buyers, sellers, and investors through every stage of the transaction.

Who Dominic Jabro Is

Based out of Downtown San Diego, Dominic Jabro (DRE #02135294) built his practice around a simple idea: real estate decisions of this size deserve a strategist, not just an agent. His work spans residential, commercial, and investment properties across North San Diego County and the surrounding region.

That range is intentional. Clients rarely fit into one category — a homeowner today may be a 1031 exchange investor tomorrow, or a commercial tenant looking to buy a multi-family property next. Dominic Jabro's practice is structured to serve that full arc, from first purchase to long-term portfolio strategy.

The firm's services touch nearly every corner of the market: retail and office leasing, industrial and development land, medical and restaurant space, residential care facilities, and the wealth management and divorce-related real estate matters that require both discretion and precision.

What Sets the Practice Apart

Most agents specialize in a single lane. Dominic Jabro's approach is deliberately broader — pairing residential market knowledge with commercial transaction experience, investment strategy, and relocation support, so clients get one point of contact instead of a rotating cast of specialists.

This matters most for clients with layered needs: an investor exploring a 1031 exchange while also relocating a family, or a business owner weighing a retail lease against a property purchase. That kind of situation calls for someone who understands the full transaction — not just one side of it.
