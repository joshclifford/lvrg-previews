Who Dominic Jabro Is

Dominic Jabro is a licensed California real estate professional based in Downtown San Diego / Civic Center, working out of offices on B Street in the heart of the city's financial and civic core.

His practice spans luxury residential homes, everyday buyer and seller transactions, and sophisticated commercial real estate investments — including retail, office, industrial, multi-family, and medical office properties. That breadth means clients get one trusted advisor whether they're buying a first home, selling a family property, or structuring a commercial acquisition.

Dominic's approach centers on market knowledge specific to San Diego's neighborhoods — from Downtown condos to North County estates — paired with the transaction discipline required for higher-stakes deals like 1031 exchanges, relocations, and investment portfolios.
