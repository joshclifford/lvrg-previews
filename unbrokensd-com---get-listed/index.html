Unbroken Fitness San Diego, Personal Training & Group Fitness: Built by a Marine Combat Veteran for Clients Who Want the Full Journey

Unbroken Fitness San Diego was founded by Eugene Neal, a United States Marine combat veteran who brought more than 27 years in the fitness industry into a studio built around one idea: that leveling up your body changes every other part of your life too, from relationships to career to finances.

Based at 11605B Duenda Rd in San Diego, the studio offers one-on-one personal training, both in-person and online, along with group fitness classes, nutrition counseling, and behavior change coaching. Eugene and his team work from a client's medical background, fitness history, goals, obstacles, home life, and nutritional habits before building a plan, rather than handing over a generic workout sheet.

If you're ready to start, the next step is a free consultation where they build a plan around your actual goals rather than a standard template.
