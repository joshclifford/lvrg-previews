Who We Are

A Mexican Bakery Made for the Neighborhood

For more than five years, Su Pan Bakery has kept the ovens warm on El Cajon Blvd, right in the heart of City Heights. What started as a small, family-run panadería has grown into a beloved stop for anyone craving conchas fresh out of the oven, a hearty torta, or a slice of cake made to order — but the spirit hasn't changed. We're still a hole-in-the-wall bakery in the truest, best sense: unpretentious, hardworking, and proud of it.

Every day we bake traditional Mexican pastries and breads alongside savory favorites — tamales, tacos, breakfast sandwiches, and fresh-squeezed orange juice — using the same quality ingredients and techniques passed down through generations. As one reviewer put it, we're "a bakery, but also offer savory food items" — that mix is exactly what makes a visit here different from a stop at any other bake shop.

Today, Su Pan Bakery serves the community from two locations — San Diego and San Marcos — but our City Heights storefront is where it all began, and where the neighborhood still gathers on weekend mornings for breakfast favorites and a warm conversation over coffee.
