Our Story

Rooted in Agave, Built on Craft

Cantina Mayahuel is a Mexican restaurant and spirits bar on Adams Ave in San Diego, built around one idea: agave deserves reverence. From the first pour of tequila to the last drop of mezcal, every bottle behind our bar is chosen with intention. We serve honest Mexican food alongside a curated spirits list, in a room designed for lingering — indoors by candlelight or outside where the evening air does the rest.

A Cantina, Not a Chain

Mexican cooking and spirits service built around an authentic cantina spirit — upscale in its offerings, unpretentious in its welcome.

Agave, Studied Closely

Our tequila and mezcal selections are chosen with the same care a sommelier gives wine — this is a bar for people who want to learn what they're drinking.

Casa Mayahuel Next Door

Fall in love with a bottle at the bar? Walk next door to Casa Mayahuel, our dedicated agave spirits shop, and take it home.
