Our Services

A full Mexican cantina experience at 2934 Adams Ave — from the dining room to the agave shelves of Casa Mayahuel next door.

01

Mexican Restaurant

Traditional plates built for sharing, served in an indoor dining room warmed by low light and aged wood, or out on the patio under the San Diego evening air.

02

Tequila Bar

A deep, curated tequila list poured by a bar staff that treats the spirit with the same respect a sommelier gives a bottle of wine.

03

Mezcal Bar

Smoky, small-batch mezcals from producers across Oaxaca and beyond, chosen for depth of flavor rather than familiarity.
