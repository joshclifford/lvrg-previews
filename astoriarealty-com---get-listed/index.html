Astoria Realty: The Boutique Brokerage Connecting Astoria, LIC and Beyond

Astoria Realty is a full-service boutique real estate brokerage based right in Astoria, Queens, built around residential sales, new development marketing and apartment rentals across Astoria, Long Island City, Brooklyn and Manhattan. The brokerage runs on a large portfolio of exclusive listings and established landlord relationships, which lets its agents connect sellers and landlords with qualified buyers and renters quickly.

The approach here is to take the time-consuming part of selling or finding a new home off a client's plate and turn it into a simple process with no hidden fees or surprises. That focus on local knowledge and direct relationships is what the team leans on for both sides of a deal, whether it's marketing a new development or finding a tenant the right apartment.

If you're ready to buy, sell, rent or list, the next step is a consultation with a local specialist who knows the building, the block and the market around it.
