Pullman Club Coffee: Metropolis Espresso and a Quiet Spot on the Historic Pullman Streets

Pullman Club Coffee sits right in the historic Pullman neighborhood, pouring Metropolis Coffee espresso and drip drinks alongside Rishi teas and a changing lineup of light snacks. It's built as a stop along the walk, a place to grab something warm before or after you've seen the historic streets around it. The menu stays close to a few things done well: a house blend for drip, a house espresso blend for everything milk-based, nitro cold brew for the heat, and a rotating stock of local pastries and donuts to go with it. Nothing elaborate, just coffee and tea brewed to order and snacks sourced from nearby bakeries. If you're planning a visit to Pullman, the shop is set up to be part of that walk: order a cup, pick up a pastry, and head back out to the neighborhood's historic blocks.

What to Order on Your First Visit

Start with the Metropolis House Blend over ice with a splash of cream, or go straight for the Red Line espresso if you want something more concentrated. For something less common, the nitro cold brew is worth trying: nitrous oxide gives it a creamier, almost stout-like texture rather than a standard iced coffee.

Pair whatever you order with whatever's come in that day from Old Fashioned Donuts or Olympia Bakery. The snack case changes regularly, so what's on the counter when you walk in is usually worth asking about.

Plan Your Visit

Pullman Club Coffee is open Tuesday through Sunday for anyone making their way through the historic Pullman neighborhood and looking for a coffee or tea along the way. See the full lineup of drinks and snacks on the Pullman Club Coffee menu.
