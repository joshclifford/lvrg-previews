Inside Asnières-sur-Seine's Answer to Late-Night Cravings for Sushi, Curry and Everything Between

By There San Diego Staff · San Diego

Tucked into Asnières-sur-Seine at 20 Rue Novion, Kamala Lounge has built its menu around a simple idea: why choose between Thai, Japanese, Chinese, Vietnamese and Cambodian food when a kitchen can do all five well. The result is an Asian fusion restaurant that moves easily from sushi rolls to pad thai to a proper curry, all under one roof, with a lounge atmosphere that leans tropical and a little vibrant rather than stiff and formal.

One Kitchen, Five Cuisines, No Compromises

What sets Kamala Lounge apart isn't just the range, it's the follow-through. The menu covers Japanese sushi and rolls, Thai curry and noodle dishes, Chinese noodles and stir-fries, plus Vietnamese and Cambodian dishes that don't often show up together on the same table. That's a lot of ground for one kitchen to cover convincingly, and it's the kind of ambition that only works when each cuisine gets treated as its own discipline rather than folded into a generic "Asian" catch-all.

Kamala Lounge is an Asian fusion restaurant located in Asnières-sur-Seine offering Thai, Japanese, and Chinese cuisine, specializing in sushi, rolls, pad thai, curries, and Asian noodle dishes with both dine-in and delivery options available.

A Lounge That Works for Lunch, Dinner and the In-Between

The hours tell you something about how the neighborhood actually uses this place. Sunday through Thursday, Kamala Lounge runs 11:30 to 15:00 and again from 19:00 to 23:00. On Fridays and Saturdays, that second seating stretches to midnight. There are weekday lunch formulas for people on a schedule, a brunch service for the slower mornings, and mocktails and desserts for anyone settling in rather than grabbing food to go. It's a rare restaurant built to handle a quick weekday lunch and a long Saturday night with equal ease.

Delivery is part of the operation too, which matters in a neighborhood like Asnières-sur-Seine where not every craving lines up with a night out. Between the dine-in room and the delivery option, Kamala Lounge covers both ends of how people actually eat during the week.

What to Order and When to Show Up

For a first visit, the smart move is to lean into the range rather than fight it: a round of sushi or rolls to start, then a Thai curry or a plate of Chinese noodles to anchor the table, with a mocktail alongside if you're not driving home right after. The lunch formulas are worth knowing about if you're in the area on a weekday and want something faster without giving up the full menu's variety.

Kamala Lounge currently sits at ★ 4.7 (586 reviews), a solid number for a restaurant juggling this many cuisines under one name.

There's no online ordering or reservation system built into the site yet, which means the fastest way to lock in a table for dinner, especially on a Friday or Saturday night when service runs to midnight, is a phone call to +33 6 28 49 11 15. If delivery is more your speed, that's worth asking about directly too. Either way, the full menu and more detail on what's available lives at kamalalounge.fr.

If you're mapping out a night in Asnières-sur-Seine that doesn't require picking a single cuisine, Kamala Lounge is worth building the evening around. Call ahead for the table, or visit Kamala Lounge's website to see the full menu before you go.
