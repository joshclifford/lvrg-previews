Our Story

A Kitchen Rooted in Place

Where theater meets table in Gennevilliers

Founded 2021

The Vision of Guillaume Chupeau

In 2021, Chef Guillaume Chupeau transformed a corner of the Théâtre de Gennevilliers (T2G) into something extraordinary — a restaurant where market-driven cuisine meets cultural vibrancy.

Ventrus L'Atelier was born from a simple conviction: exceptional dining should be accessible, not exclusive. Here, fine dining sensibilities merge with the warmth of a neighborhood gathering place.

The name "L'Atelier" reflects our philosophy — this kitchen is a workshop where creativity flourishes daily, guided by what's freshest at market and what's ready to harvest from our rooftop garden above the theater.

Our Philosophy

What Sets Us Apart

Rooftop Garden

Our vegetables travel just a few flights — from the theater's rooftop garden directly to your plate. No food miles here.

Short Supply Chains

We partner with local producers who share our commitment to quality. Every ingredient has a story, and we know them all.

Seasonal Respect

Our menu changes with the seasons because nature knows best. What's ripe today becomes tonight's inspiration.

Cultural Integration

Dining at the Theater

Our location inside the Théâtre de Gennevilliers isn't incidental — it's intentional. We believe that nourishment of the body and spirit belong together.

Whether you're joining us before a performance, catching up over afterwork tapas, or simply exploring Gennevilliers' creative quarter, our doors are open to anyone who appreciates thoughtfully prepared food.

The theater's energy infuses everything we do. Art on stage, art on the plate — both demand presence, attention, and care.
