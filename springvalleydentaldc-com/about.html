About Spring Valley Dental

Family dentistry, rooted in the Spring Valley neighborhood

Led by doctors who treat patients like family

Spring Valley Dental was built around a simple idea: dental care should feel personal, not clinical. Under the shared leadership of Dr. Priya Grewal and Dr. Steve Philips, our Washington, D.C. practice combines modern technology with a minimally-invasive, patient-first philosophy — treating only what needs treating, and explaining every step along the way.

We see patients of every age, from first checkups to full restorative care, and we built our practice at 4900 Massachusetts Ave NW specifically to serve the families of Spring Valley and the surrounding D.C. community with convenience, warmth, and clinical excellence in one place.

That's why our care spans everything from routine hygiene visits to cosmetic dentistry, Invisalign, implants, root canals, and emergency care — so your whole family can get comprehensive treatment without being sent elsewhere.

Trusted across Washington, D.C.

Our patient-first approach and commitment to comfortable, minimally-invasive care have earned the trust of families throughout Spring Valley and beyond.

What makes our approach different

We built Spring Valley Dental to remove the stress from dental visits — from the moment you reach out to the moment you leave with a healthier smile.

Minimally-invasive philosophy

We prioritize conservative, comfortable treatment options before recommending anything more involved — preserving as much of your natural smile as possible.

Two doctors, one shared standard

Dr. Grewal and Dr. Philips lead a collaborative practice, so every patient benefits from aligned expertise across preventative, restorative, and cosmetic care.

Easier to reach, easier to book

We're working to make scheduling simpler — with clearer booking confirmations and new ways to stay in touch, so you always know your appointment is set.

Stay connected to your care
