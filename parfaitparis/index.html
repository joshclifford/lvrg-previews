Our Story

A Taste of Paris in
Southern California

The story of Parfait Paris started in 2014 with the opening of a family-run Pâtisserie & Boulangerie in San Diego's historic Gaslamp Quarter.

Today, Parfait Paris brings its French savoir-faire throughout Southern California, delivering authentic Parisian experiences to dessert lovers across the region. Every macaron, every croissant, every creation is made from scratch with love and expertise.
