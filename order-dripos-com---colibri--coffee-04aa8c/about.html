Who We Are

The Hummingbird
of Chicago's Coffee Scene

The name "Colibri"—Spanish for hummingbird—captures everything we believe in: energy, resilience, and the sweet moments that make life beautiful. Like the hummingbird, we're small but mighty, bringing joy to our corner of Chicago at 3639 E 106th St.

We're not just another coffee shop. We're your morning ritual, your afternoon escape, your neighborhood gathering place. Every cup we serve is a connection—to our community, to quality, to the simple pleasure of something made with care.
