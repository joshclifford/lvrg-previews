Who We Are

A Neighborhood Brasserie with Heart

Nestled on Avenue de la Paix in the Quartier de la Paix of Asnières-sur-Seine, Chouchou was born from a simple belief: that great food brings people together. We're not just a restaurant — we're a gathering place where neighbors become friends and every meal feels like coming home.

Our kitchen crafts fresh, homemade cuisine from morning to midnight. Whether you're stopping by for a quick lunch between meetings, lingering over dinner with friends, or enjoying our beloved weekend brunch, you'll find the same dedication to quality and warmth that defines the Chouchou experience.

With multiple spaces including our cozy dining room, summer and winter terraces, and a welcoming bar, there's always a perfect spot waiting for you — whatever the occasion, whatever the season.
