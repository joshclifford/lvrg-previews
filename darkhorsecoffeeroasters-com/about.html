Who We Are

Specialty Coffee with a Quirky Soul

Dark Horse Coffee Roasters isn't just another coffee shop — we're a specialty coffee roaster born and bred in San Diego. From our home base at 811 25th St, we source, roast, and curate single-origin and blended coffees that tell a story with every sip.

Our roasting team obsesses over quality and availability, working directly with farmers through direct trade partnerships. This means the coffee in your cup isn't just delicious — it's ethically sourced and supports sustainable farming communities around the world.

Whether you're a pour-over purist, an espresso enthusiast, or someone who just loves a really good cup of coffee on a slow Saturday morning, we've got something special waiting for you.
