Our Story

Rooted in San Diego,
Reaching the World

Dark Horse Coffee Roasters began with a simple belief: exceptional coffee should be accessible to everyone. From our home base in San Diego's vibrant Normal Heights neighborhood, we've built a specialty coffee company that honors both the craft of roasting and the farmers who make it all possible.

We source single-origin coffees directly from farmers in Guatemala, Ethiopia, Colombia, and Brazil — building relationships that go beyond transactions. Direct trade isn't just a label for us; it's a commitment to transparency, fair compensation, and sustainable farming practices.

Every batch is roasted in-house with meticulous attention to detail, bringing out the unique characteristics that make each origin special. Whether you prefer bright and fruity Ethiopian naturals or smooth Colombian classics, we're here to guide your coffee journey.

Growing Together

From Local Favorite to Beloved Brand

What started as a passion project has grown into something truly special. With over 34,000 followers on Instagram and a loyal community spanning from our Adams Avenue headquarters to the shores of Kaua'i, Dark Horse has become a destination for coffee lovers seeking something beyond the ordinary.

Our wholesale program partners with cafes and restaurants who share our commitment to quality, while our subscription service brings freshly roasted beans directly to homes across the country. Whether you prefer whole bean, French press, pour over, or espresso grind, we've got you covered.

And for those who love variety? Our Roaster's Choice program puts discovery at the heart of your coffee routine — hand-selected seasonal offerings that showcase the best of what we're roasting.
