Who We Are

Born on Adams Avenue, Built for Coffee Lovers

Dark Horse Coffee Roasters didn't start in a boardroom or with a business plan — it started with a simple belief: that great coffee should be accessible, approachable, and absolutely delicious.

Based in the heart of San Diego's Adams Avenue neighborhood, we're a specialty coffee roaster that takes our craft seriously, but never ourselves. Our team obsesses over sourcing single-origin beans and crafting custom blends that tell a story with every sip.

We believe coffee is more than a morning ritual — it's a moment of pause, a conversation starter, and sometimes, the best part of your day. That's a responsibility we don't take lightly.

Our Values

What Makes Us Different

We're not trying to be everything to everyone. We're trying to be the best cup of coffee you've ever had.

Single-Origin Focus

We source directly from farms that share our passion for quality. Each bean has a story, and we're here to tell it through exceptional roasting.

Playful Sophistication

Specialty coffee doesn't have to be pretentious. We bring dark sophistication with a playful personality — serious about quality, never about being snobby.

Community First

Our Approach

Curated by Roasters Who Care

Our roasting team doesn't just follow recipes — they create experiences. Every batch is roasted with intention, calibrated to bring out the unique characteristics that make each origin special.

From rare one-off coffees to our carefully crafted blends, we're constantly exploring, tasting, and refining. Our Roaster's Choice subscriptions let you join us on that journey, receiving hand-picked selections based on what's tasting exceptional right now.

Whether you prefer whole bean for your weekend pour-over ritual or a fine espresso grind for your morning shot, we've got you covered with custom grind options for every brewing method.
