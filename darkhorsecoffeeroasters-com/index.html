Why Dark Horse

We go where the great coffee grows

Direct trade isn't just a label for us — it's how we do business. We build relationships with farmers, pay fair prices, and bring back coffees that tell a story. From the volcanic soils of Guatemala to the ancient growing regions of Ethiopia, every bag represents a journey.

Based in San Diego's Normal Heights neighborhood, we roast small batches to bring out the best in each origin. Whether you prefer the bright, fruity notes of an Ethiopian natural or the chocolate undertones of a Colombian washed, we've got something extraordinary waiting for you.
