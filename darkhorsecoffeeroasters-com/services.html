Our Coffee Services

Every bean tells a story. We source directly from farmers in Guatemala, Ethiopia, Colombia, and Brazil to bring you coffees with character and complexity.

Single-Origin Coffees

Experience the distinct terroir of each region. Our single-origins from Guatemala offer chocolate and citrus notes, while Ethiopian selections bring bright, fruity complexity. Colombian coffees deliver balanced sweetness, and Brazilian beans provide nutty, smooth profiles.

  • • Guatemala — Chocolate, citrus undertones
  • • Ethiopia — Bright, fruity, floral
  • • Colombia — Balanced, caramel sweetness
  • • Brazil — Nutty, smooth, low acidity

Artisan Coffee Blends

Our master roasters craft signature blends that combine the best characteristics of multiple origins. Each blend is designed to deliver a consistent, memorable cup whether you're brewing at home or serving in a cafe.

  • • Espresso blends — Rich, balanced shots
  • • House blends — Everyday excellence
  • • Seasonal offerings — Limited releases
  • • Half-caff options available

Multiple Grind Options

Not everyone has a grinder at home, and we believe everyone deserves perfectly ground coffee. We offer precise grinds tailored to your specific brewing method for optimal extraction.

  • • Whole Bean — Maximum freshness
  • • French Press — Coarse grind
  • • Drip / Pour Over — Medium grind
  • • Aeropress — Fine-medium grind
  • • Espresso — Extra fine grind

Decaf & Half-Caff Options

Great coffee shouldn't require caffeine. Our decaf offerings use the Swiss Water Process to remove caffeine while preserving the complex flavors you love. Half-caff blends give you the best of both worlds.

  • • Swiss Water Process decaf
  • • Full flavor, no compromise
  • • Half-caff custom blends
  • • Perfect for any time of day
