Custom Grind Options

Your brewing method deserves the perfect grind. We'll prepare your coffee exactly how you need it.

Whole Bean

Maximum freshness for home grinders. Grind right before brewing for peak flavor.

French Press

Coarse grind for full immersion brewing. Bold, rich, and full-bodied results.

Drip Coffee

Medium grind for automatic drip machines. Consistent extraction every time.

Pour Over

Medium-fine grind for manual brewing. Clean, bright, and nuanced cups.

Aeropress

Fine-medium grind for versatile brewing. Smooth and concentrated flavor.

Espresso

Fine grind for high-pressure extraction. Intense, syrupy, and aromatic shots.
