Common Questions

Quick answers about our coffees, subscriptions, and visiting us in San Diego

What makes Dark Horse coffees special?

We source direct trade coffees from exceptional farms in Guatemala, Ethiopia, Colombia, Brazil, and beyond. Each origin is carefully roasted to highlight its unique flavor profile — from bright, fruity naturals to rich, chocolatey blends. Our small-batch approach ensures freshness you can taste.

How does the Roaster's Choice subscription work?

With Roaster's Choice, our team selects freshly roasted coffees based on what's tasting exceptional each shipment. You'll discover new single-origins and seasonal offerings — perfect for adventurous coffee lovers who trust our expertise. Choose from 1 to 4 bags per delivery.

Do you offer wholesale for coffee shops?

Yes! We partner with cafes, restaurants, and offices throughout San Diego and beyond. Call us at (619) 344-6962 or send a message through the form above to discuss wholesale pricing, training, and equipment support.

What grind options do you offer?

We grind to order for maximum freshness: whole bean, French press, drip, pour over, Aeropress, or espresso. Not sure which to choose? We recommend whole bean if you have a grinder — but we're happy to help you decide!
