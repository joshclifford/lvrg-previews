Common Questions

Quick answers about subscriptions and orders.

How does the Roaster's Choice subscription work?

Our roasting team curates unique coffee selections based on current quality and availability. Each month, you'll receive freshly roasted beans chosen specifically for their exceptional flavor profiles. It's like getting a surprise gift from coffee-obsessed friends.

Can I choose my grind preference?

Absolutely! We offer custom grind options including whole bean, French press, drip, pour over, Aeropress, and espresso. Just let us know your preference when ordering.

Do you offer wholesale for cafes?

Yes! We provide wholesale distribution for cafes, restaurants, and retailers. Reach out using the form above or give us a call to discuss partnership opportunities.

Do you have decaf options?

We sure do! We offer both decaf and half-caff options so you can enjoy our specialty roasts any time of day without the buzz.
