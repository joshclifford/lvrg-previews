Our Story

Where Nostalgia Meets Home Cooking

Since 1995, Antique Row Cafe has been more than just a restaurant—it's a gathering place where families create memories over steaming plates of comfort food. Located in the heart of Adams Avenue, our cozy dining room and outdoor patio have welcomed generations of San Diegans.

Every dish tells a story of tradition, from our beloved California omelette to our legendary Philly Steak. We're proud to be family-owned and deeply rooted in our community, blending classic American style with modern comfort.
