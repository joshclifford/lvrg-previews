Who We Are

Traditional Baking, Rooted in Carlsbad

Prager Brothers Artisan Breads began with a simple belief: bread deserves time. Long before "artisan" became a buzzword, we were mixing dough by hand, letting it ferment slowly, and baking in small batches — because that's what makes a crust worth breaking.

Today, our Carlsbad kitchen still runs on that same principle. Every loaf, pastry, and pizza we make starts with quality ingredients and the patience of traditional long fermentation — never shortcuts, never fillers.

We serve Southern California from multiple pickup locations, but our heart is in the neighborhood ovens where it all comes together, loaf by loaf, one careful batch at a time.
