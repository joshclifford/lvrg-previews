Who We Are

An ESAT with a Purpose

Ateliers de la Boucle de la Seine (ATBS) is an Établissement et Service d'Aide par le Travail (ESAT) — an inclusive work facility dedicated to providing meaningful employment opportunities for people with disabilities in the heart of Gennevilliers.

Since our founding, we've believed that everyone deserves the dignity of work and the chance to contribute their talents. Our diverse team brings dedication, precision, and genuine care to every service we provide — from crafting exquisite catering experiences to maintaining beautiful green spaces.

When you choose ATBS, you're not just getting professional service — you're supporting a mission that transforms lives and strengthens our community.
