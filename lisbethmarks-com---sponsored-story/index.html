Lisbeth Marks, REALTOR®: Del Mar Heights Agent Mapping San Diego's Coast and Canyons

By There San Diego Staff · San Diego

Ask most people to describe San Diego real estate and they will give you one neighborhood, maybe two. Lisbeth Marks, REALTOR® works across the whole map, from oceanfront stretches to inland investment pockets, out of an office at 3525 Del Mar Heights Rd in Del Mar Heights. That range, more than any single listing, is what her clients come back for.

Del Mar Heights sits in a part of the county where the real estate conversation never really stops. New buyers are comparing school districts, longtime owners are wondering what their home is worth now, and investors are quietly watching which communities are about to turn over. Lisbeth works all three conversations at once, representing individual families and corporate clients who need someone fluent in California real estate at every price point.

A practice built around the whole county, not one zip code

Plenty of agents specialize in a neighborhood and stop there. Lisbeth's specialty is San Diego area properties broadly, luxury homes, oceanfront properties, and investment opportunities across multiple communities. That breadth means a client who buys a starter investment property today and wants a beachfront home in five years never has to start over with someone new.

Expertise in buying, selling, and investing in California real estate for both individual and corporate clients.

The service list reads like a full real estate practice rather than a single specialty: buying a home, selling a home, home evaluations, featured listings, community searches, financing options, and investment properties. For a first-time buyer trying to understand a neighborhood, or a seller who just wants a straight answer on value before listing, that range matters. It means one phone call covers a lot of ground.

What first-time clients actually use

The most common starting point is the simplest one. A homeowner curious about current value can request a free home evaluation and get a real read on the market rather than a guess. Buyers who are still exploring tend to start with the community searches, using the geographic knowledge to narrow down which part of San Diego actually fits their life, then move into financing options once a direction is clear.

Investment clients, meanwhile, lean on the same local knowledge from a different angle, looking at which communities are positioned for growth and which featured listings fit a portfolio rather than a personal move. It is the same agent, the same office in Del Mar Heights, just a different conversation.

Where to start

The practical first step is low-pressure: a free home evaluation if you own property and want to know where you stand, or a consultation if you are weighing a purchase, a sale, or an investment and want someone who can speak to all three. Either way, the office is easy to find in Del Mar Heights, and the full range of services is laid out on her website.

For anyone ready to take that step, the details below cover how to reach her, and lisbethmarks.com has the rest.
