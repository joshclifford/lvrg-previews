About Lisbeth

A San Diego REALTOR® built on trust, not transactions.

Based in Del Mar Heights, Lisbeth Marks has spent years guiding buyers and sellers across San Diego County through some of the biggest financial decisions of their lives — with the professionalism of a specialist and the care of a neighbor.

Representing buyers, sellers, and investors across San Diego

Lisbeth Marks is a REALTOR® specializing in San Diego area real estate, serving both individual families and corporate clients throughout California. From first-time buyers navigating the market to sellers positioning a home for top dollar, her approach is rooted in preparation, clear communication, and an intimate knowledge of San Diego's neighborhoods — including her own home base, Del Mar Heights.

Her work spans the full real estate journey: home buying and selling strategy, property search by price range, in-depth community guides, home evaluations, financing guidance, and a continually updated slate of featured listings. Clients get a single point of contact who understands both the numbers and the neighborhoods.
